## 🧬 About us

The **Chromatin and Aging Research Lab (CARL)** is led by **Prof. Michael Klutstein** at the Institute of Dental Sciences, Faculty of Dental Medicine, **The Hebrew University of Jerusalem**.

Our main interest is **epigenetic aging**: the gradual changes in how DNA is packaged that build up as cells get older. We focus on the reproductive system (oocytes and sperm) and on the oral immune system. We also study how cells adapt when their environment changes over long periods, as it does during aging.

## 🔬 Research themes

| | Theme | The question |
|:---:|---|---|
| 🥚 | **Aging of the germline** | How do heterochromatin and histone modifications change as oocytes and sperm age, and can those changes be slowed to protect fertility? |
| 🦷 | **Oral immunity and aging** | How does age affect inflammation and immune responses in the mouth? We use a mouse model of apical periodontitis and study clinical samples. |
| 🍺 | **Survival under long-term stress** | How do yeast and other eukaryotic cells survive years of starvation, and how does this compare with the "growth advantage in stationary phase" seen in bacteria? |
| 🏺 | **Yeast from ancient vessels** | Can live yeast be recovered from ancient beer vessels, and what do their genomes tell us about how they survived so long? |
